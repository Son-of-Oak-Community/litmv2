# Hero Sheet (Cards)

Pick **Hero Sheet (Cards)** in the hero sheet's **Configure Sheet** menu.

## The board

- **Header:** portrait, name, Promise, "What do you do?", and the **Roll!**,
  **Actions**, **Sacrifice** and **Notes** buttons.
- **Statuses and Backpack first.** They change every scene, and statuses count
  toward Power, so they sit above the themes.
- **One card per theme**, coloured by might: Origin green, Adventure red,
  Greatness blue, Fellowship gold.
- **On each card:** one tag per line (weaknesses marked), the Abandon / Improve
  / Milestone tracks, the **Quest**, and **Special Improvements**.
- **Fellowship card** carries the Fellowship theme and your relationship tags.

---

## Banner icons are buttons

For the hero's owner, in Play and Edit mode:

| Icon | Does |
|---|---|
| A theme's icon | Opens that theme's sheet |
| Fellowship | Opens the Fellowship sheet |
| Backpack | Opens the backpack |
| Statuses droplet | Adds a status |

A viewer sees the plain icons, with no buttons.

Double-clicking a banner does the same as its icon. In Play mode, the Statuses
and Moment of Fulfillment banners switch the sheet to Edit mode instead.

---

## Narrow windows

Below the wide breakpoint the sheet stacks: the portrait becomes a banner
across the top, and the cards wrap into rows.

---

## Portrait panel

On a wide sheet the portrait becomes a side panel with the name, Promise and
description. The arrow button folds it to a thumbnail so the cards get the
width.

- The choice is remembered per browser, for every Cards sheet you open.
- A hero with no portrait and no description starts folded.
- Without a portrait, the owner clicks the framed placeholder to pick one.

---

## Dark theme

Set **Theme: Dark** in Configure Sheet. Cards are printed on parchment, light
or dark to match the theme, and the canvas shows faintly through the window.

---

## Edit mode

Press `E` or use the header button. Every card shows all its tags, with the
unused ones dimmed, plus a delete button on the banner. Locked special
improvements show how to unlock them. Statuses and backpack tags become
editable lists, and the description opens for writing.

---

## Flip and reorder

The rotate button on a card's banner turns it over to show the theme's note.
The card keeps its height, and stays flipped while you play.

Drag a card by its banner to move it along the row. The Fellowship card can
move too; its place is saved for this hero.

---

## Colour-blind safe

With **Colorblind-safe Palette** on (Game Settings), the might colours switch
to hues that stay distinct under red-green and blue-yellow colour blindness.
The Statuses and Backpack strips keep their sienna.
